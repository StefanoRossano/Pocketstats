Pocket Stats - Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

1. Introduction

Pocket Stats ("the App") is a statistics and tracking application for Pokémon TCG Pocket gameplay.

This Privacy Policy explains how Pocket Stats handles user data. The App is designed to operate locally on the user's device and does not require an account or a connection to a server operated by the developer.

2. Data Collection

Pocket Stats does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data with the developer or with third parties.

The App does not require users to create an account and does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or similar personal information.

3. Data Stored on the Device

Information entered by the user, such as decks, matches, seasons, statistics, and related game-tracking information, is stored locally on the user's device.

This information is used solely to provide the functionality of the App.

4. Images

The App may allow the user to select images from the device for use within the App. These images are selected by the user and are used locally by the App.

Pocket Stats does not upload these images to a server operated by the developer.

5. Backup and Device Transfer

Pocket Stats stores its application data locally. Depending on the user's Android device and Google account settings, Android system backup and device-transfer features may back up or restore application data.

Such backup and restoration are controlled by the Android operating system and the user's device settings and are not operated by the Pocket Stats developer.

6. Data Sharing

Pocket Stats does not share user data with the developer or with third parties.

The App does not contain advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or a developer- operated backend for collecting user data.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Because Pocket Stats does not collect or store user data on developer- operated servers, the developer does not retain a copy of the user's application data.

Users can delete their locally stored Pocket Stats data by deleting the data of the App or by uninstalling the App from their device. Android backup and restoration behavior may depend on the device and the user's system settings.

8. Children's Privacy

Pocket Stats does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users because the App does not collect personal information through a developer-operated service.

9. Third-Party Services

Pocket Stats does not use third-party services to collect analytics, advertising data, or personal information from users.

The App may use Android and Google Play system functionality required for installation, updates, backup, and device operation. These services are provided and governed by their respective providers.

10. Security

Pocket Stats is designed to keep user-generated application data on the user's device. The developer does not operate a server that receives or stores this data.

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their device and their device or Google account credentials.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary to reflect changes to the App or applicable requirements. The date shown at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last updated.
